# Code of Conduct

DirectPilot follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, adapted for a small beta project.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Keep issues and PRs focused on the technical problem.
- Assume good intent, but require evidence for safety-sensitive changes.
- Do not post secrets, private account data, or customer data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private credentials or customer data.
- Pressuring maintainers to merge unsafe or untested changes.
- Using the project to perform unauthorized actions against third-party systems.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; reject PRs; or block users who violate this code.
